# C Bitwise Operations - Questions & Answers

This file contains my practice questions and answers on Bitwise Operations in C.

## MCQs


### 1. What are all the bitwise operators available in C?

a) `|`, `&`, `~`, `^`, `>>`, `*`  
b) `||`, `&&`, `^`, `>>`, `<=`  
c) `|`, `&`, `^`, `<=`, `>>`  
d) `|`, `&`, `^`, `~`, `>>`, `<<`

**Answer:** d) `|`, `&`, `^`, `~`, `>>`, `<<`

### 2. What is the primary use of bitwise operators in C?

a) To initialize a variable  
b) To confuse the programmer  
c) To access and modify data in the context of bits  
d) To perform mathematical operations

**Answer:** c) To access and modify data in the context of bits

### 3. What is the OR bitwise operator in C?

a) `||`  
b) `&&`  
c) `&`  
d) `|`

**Answer:** d) `|`

### 4. What is the primary difference between left and right shift (`<<`, `>>`)?

a) Both are same  
b) For signed types, after right shift, the MSB may be filled with the sign bit  
c) For unsigned types, after right shift, the MSB is filled with the sign bit  
d) For signed types, after left shift, the LSB is filled with the sign bit

**Answer:** b) For signed types, after right shift, the MSB may be filled with the sign bit
