# C Bitwise Operations - Questions & Answers

This file contains my practice questions and answers on Bitwise Operations in C.

## MCQs

### 1. What are all the bitwise operators available in C?

a) `|`, `&`, `~`, `^`, `>>`, `*`  
b) `||`, `&&`, `^`, `>>`, `<=`  
c) `|`, `&`, `^`, `<=`, `>>`  
d) `|`, `&`, `^`, `~`, `>>`, `<<`

**Answer:** d) `|`, `&`, `^`, `~`, `>>`, `<<`
